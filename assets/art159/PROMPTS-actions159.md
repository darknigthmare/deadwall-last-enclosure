# Actor action originals — 1.59

The exact prompts and historical image references for the seventeen accepted transparent ImageGen originals are recorded alongside their byte hashes:

- `PROVENANCE-actions-allies159.json`: six sources, four survivor roles, six actions and two existing appearances per action; 96 used drawings. The medic original contains eight additional explicitly unused drawings.
- `PROVENANCE-actions-infected159.json`: eleven sources, ten existing infected kinds, two existing appearances per kind and 160 used attack drawings. Each crawler appearance has its own eight-pose original.

`PROVENANCE-actions159.json` is the final registry and qualification index. These are 32 eight-frame action cycles (256 used poses), preserving 28 historical appearances across fourteen existing roles. They are not new unit types.

No generated image pixels were edited. Native whole-body cuts, uniform scale calibration and fixed pivots preserve the physical owner through the animation. The current walking appearance selects its matching action appearance, including the historical distinction between overhead and side views. The shielded body contains no baked shield; the native equipment overlay supplies its existing shield separately.

The first nine proposed originals failed costume/camera comparison and are not shipped. Their raw files, exact prompts and SHA-256 manifest remain in the session archive `/workspace/deadwall-cloud/audit159/action-candidates-early/` as negative evidence. They are not counted as accepted sprites or runtime action frames.
